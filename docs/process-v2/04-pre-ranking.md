## The attractor problem, stated plainly

Six of the top sixteen share a shape: **an instrument or record that goes blind exactly where it matters, and
whose blindness hides itself.** They are the fold-back signal, settledness trap, remedy–signature collision, open
top bracket, all-clear trap, and partly placeholder activation. Several helpers working from unrelated seeds landed
there, and two found the *same* concept (fold-back / pseudonormalization). That convergence suggests the pattern is
real and common in the world. It doesn't show the territory is unexplored. Goodhart, Campbell, legibility and
survivorship already sit nearby, and the requester knows that neighbourhood well.

**Consequence for choosing:** I won't pick two concepts from this family. A pair like fold-back + settledness would
read as one idea in two costumes ("silence is ambiguous"). At most one concept comes from the family; the other
should come from outside it. Of the strong candidates, **refractory-check capture** (strategy, timing,
self-administered tests) is the clearest outsider. Placeholder activation is half in, half out: its mechanism is
strategic slot-filling, not instrument physics.
