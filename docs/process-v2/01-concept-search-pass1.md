# 01 — Concept search, pass 1 (my own sweep, before helpers)

The goal was to find candidates. Each one was checked against the four tests (transfer, new moves, surplus, not
already named). No game considerations at this stage.

## What I swept

- **Doctrines and practices:** law (property, probate, torts, tax), insurance (IBNR, credibility, subrogation,
  insurable interest), surveying and maps (datums, priority of calls, the original surveyor's footsteps, trap
  streets, wild deeds).
- **Tacit craft rules:** woodworking, sewing, cooking, painting, masonry, sailing, climbing, magic, beekeeping,
  Go and fencing.
- **Practitioner maxims** from about 40 professions.
- **Formal structures** applied to life: holonomy/commutators, gauge freedom, monodromy, invariants,
  percolation, frustration.
- **Inversions** of known principles (anti-Goodhart, inverse Chesterton, etc.).
- **"New cognitive operations"** in the style of the veil of ignorance or the pre-mortem.

## Lessons for pass 2

- My free association lands on named territory fast. Practitioner wisdom has mostly been named by practitioners
  already.
- The survivors of a real search are likely to be one of two kinds: (a) a structure that recurs across fields,
  where each field has at most a local name and nobody has noticed they're the same thing, provided the general
  version yields moves the local versions don't; or (b) something stranger that I can't reach by free association.
- To diversify, pass 2 gives helpers narrow, concrete domain seeds instead of a general brief. I'll treat any
  convergence between them with suspicion.
